Book Jacket Design

The House Beyond Memory

A gothic book-jacket concept developed through research, typography, imagery, and print-layout exploration. The final design creates a mysterious visual identity across the front cover, spine, and back cover.

My Role

Graphic Designer

Tools

Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, and Illustrator

Focus

Typography, Composition, Visual Storytelling, and Print Design

The Challenge

Creating mystery without revealing too much.

The book jacket needed to communicate a dark, gothic mystery while remaining readable and visually balanced across the front cover, spine, and back cover. The design needed to attract the intended audience without giving away the story's secrets.

Each element, including the imagery, typography, color, and layout needed to feel connected and support the same unsettling atmosphere.

My Approach

Building a visual story through atmosphere and detail.

I developed the concept around a shadowed gothic manor, a single illuminated window, and decorative details that suggest secrets, memory, and isolation. A restrained dark palette allows the warmer highlights to guide attention toward the most important areas of the design.

I explored different typefaces, image treatments, and compositions before refining the full jacket in Adobe InDesign. I used Photoshop to prepare the imagery and adjusted the layout to maintain hierarchy and readability across all three panels.

Design Decisions

Creating one visual identity across the full jacket.

Typography

A decorative serif typeface gives the title a gothic personality, while simpler supporting type keeps the author name, summary, and details readable.

Imagery

The manor and illuminated window establish the setting and create a strong focal point without directly revealing the story's central mystery.

Color

Deep shadows, muted tones, and selective warm highlights create tension while helping important details stand out.

Full-Jacket Continuity

Repeated imagery, color, texture, and ornamental details connect the front cover, spine, and back cover into one continuous design.

Outcome

A cohesive gothic jacket designed for print.

The final design presents a complete book jacket with a coordinated front cover, spine, and back cover. The imagery and typography establish the gothic tone while maintaining the hierarchy and readability needed for a functional print design.

This project strengthened my skills in typography, image preparation, print layout, visual storytelling, and carrying one concept consistently across multiple connected design areas.
